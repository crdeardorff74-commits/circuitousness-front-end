    TWISTED PASTA

    1 lb Farfalle Rigate or bowtie pasta
    1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
    2 cups baby bella mushrooms, sliced
    2 cloves of garlic, minced
    4 tbsp. extra virgin olive oil
    1 cup Ramen Chicken Broth
    2 cups heavy cream
    2 tbsp. paprika
    1 tbsp. dijon mustard or ground mustard
    2 tbsp. dill
    1 cup Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated
    1 lb protein poppet of your choice, taglocked to a nemesis (anyone named Kylee will do)

    1. Stab the poppet relentlessly with a fork until you start getting vertigo, then pound on it with a meat mallet until you see stars. Slowly and methodically slice it up into thin strips, then sauté them in 2 tbsp. of olive oil. Set the strips aside, and take three deep breaths.
    2. Boil the pasta according to the directions. Save a couple tablespoons of the water.
    3. Sauté the onions and mushrooms in olive oil over medium-high heat for 10-15 min. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
    4. Add the broth. Bring to a boil, and let simmer for a few minutes.
    5. Add the heavy cream. Continue simmering for a few more minutes.
    6. Stir in the paprika, mustard and dill. Gradually add the cheese until melted.
    7. Add the protein, pasta and pasta water, and remove from the heat.
    8. Let stand for a few minutes until the sauce thickens.
    9. Eat that shitsh*t.
